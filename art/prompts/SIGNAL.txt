[STYLE]
Late-1990s real-time 3D, remembered fondly. Deliberately low polygon count with
un-smoothed facets, tiny 64x64 textures whose pixels are clearly visible, no
normal maps, and hard ordered-dither patterning standing in for gradients and
fog. Vertex-coloured surfaces, unlit or simply vertex-lit. Palette is
high-contrast and slightly wrong -- cyan, magenta, amber and deep blue-black --
and colour banding is a feature rather than an artefact.
