[STYLE]
Hand-painted storybook illustration in gouache, like a 1970s children's picture
book. Visible brush strokes and low texel density -- the paint should read as
paint. Lighting is soft and low-contrast so it never fights the painted
surface; painted-in shadow pools where objects meet the ground. Palette is
dusty and warm: ochre, sage, brick and cream, against a cool sky that pushes the
subject forward. Saturation is rationed and spent only on things that threaten
you.
