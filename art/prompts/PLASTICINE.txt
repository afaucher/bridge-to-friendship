Stop-motion clay animation, Aardman-like. Everything is modelled from
plasticine: matte clay with a faint waxy sheen, visible thumb dents, fingerprint
texture and sculpting-tool marks. Seams show where two colours of clay were
pressed together. Nothing is perfectly straight or symmetrical -- every edge is
hand-formed and slightly wobbly. Colours are warm and a little dirty, because
clay never stays clean: an earthy base with three or four saturated accents that
read as freshly-worked clay. Lit like a miniature set on a table with two soft
lamps, shallow warm shadows.
