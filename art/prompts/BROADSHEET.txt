[STYLE]
Flat inked comic art, ligne claire. Two-band toon shading with absolutely no
specular highlight and no gradient anywhere: shadow is a hatched or dot-screen
pattern, not a soft falloff. Every object carries a heavy black ink contour, and
line weight encodes importance -- thick on characters and threats, thin on
scenery. Colour is a limited printed set: paper-white ground, one dense ink
black, and six to eight flat spot colours with a hint of print
misregistration. Reads as a panel from a comic that happens to be
three-dimensional.
