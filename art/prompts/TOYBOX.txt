[STYLE]
Injection-moulded plastic playset. Every object is a piece from the same toy
set: glossy ABS plastic, high specular, low roughness, no metal anywhere. Soft
2 mm chamfers on every edge, because a mould cannot make a sharp corner. Visible
mould seam lines and leftover sprue nubs. Sticker decals applied very slightly
crooked. Wear appears as scuffing and stress-whitening at the corners, never as
rust or dirt. Palette is a strict six-colour toy set -- red, blue, yellow,
green, white, black -- plus one neutral grey for the baseplate; no colour sits
between two of those. Clean bright even studio light, soft shadow.
